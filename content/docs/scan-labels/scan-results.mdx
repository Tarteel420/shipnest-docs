---
title: Scan results
description: What each scan status means.
---

The **Scanned in this session** panel keeps a running list and four counters.

<Screenshot src="/images/scan-labels/scan-labels-notfound.png" alt="A scan with status Not Found" caption="An unknown tracking number shows Not Found and a warning message." />

| Status | Meaning | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Processed** | The tracking number matches a shipment and has been recorded. | Nothing — the order leaves To Process. |
| **Duplicate** | This tracking number was already scanned in this session. | Check you haven't scanned the same package twice. |
| **Not Found** | The tracking number doesn't match any shipment (*Invalid or unknown tracking number*). | Re-scan, or check the label belongs to this account. |
| **Total** | Number of scans in this session. | — |
