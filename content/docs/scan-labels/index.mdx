---
title: Scan Labels
description: Confirm packages before carrier pickup by scanning their tracking numbers.
---

**Scan Labels** is the warehouse verification screen. Scan (or type) each package's tracking number before the carrier collects it. A successful scan removes the order from **To Process**; it stays in **Shipped**.

Users with the **Warehouse** role only see this page.

<Screenshot src="/images/scan-labels/scan-labels-empty.png" alt="Label Scan page" />

## Scan a label

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open Scan Labels

Click **Scan Labels** in the sidebar.

</Step>
<Step>

### Scan the barcode

Click in **Tracking Number** and scan the label's barcode with a scanner, or type the tracking number.

</Step>
<Step>

### Submit

Press **Enter** (most scanners do this automatically) or click **Scan**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Check the result

The scan appears in **Scanned in this session** with its status. See [Scan results](/docs/scan-labels/scan-results).

</Step>
</Steps>

Click **Clear** under the field to empty it. Use **Clear** (trash icon) in the session panel to reset the session list and counters.

<Callout>
  If the carrier confirms pickup before a label is scanned, the order also leaves **To Process** automatically. To skip this step entirely, see [Skip To Process step](/docs/account-settings/shipping-and-rates#skip-to-process-step).
</Callout>
