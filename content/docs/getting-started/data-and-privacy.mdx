---
title: Your data and privacy
description: How ShipNest keeps each organization's data separate.
---

Every organization (tenant) in ShipNest can only see and change **its own** data. Other organizations' orders, labels, accounts and settings are never shown to you, and you can't change them — whether you use the ShipNest screens or connect to ShipNest directly.

## What is protected

Organization separation covers every area that holds your data:

| Area | What only your organization can see and change |
| --- | --- |
| **Shipments** | Adding, deleting, cancelling and reprinting shipments |
| **Shipping labels** | Labels, which can only use carrier accounts of the same organization |
| **Orders** | Viewing, cloning, connecting with other systems and bulk shipping |
| **Order items** | The items on each order |
| **Packages** | The packages in each shipment |
| **Carrier accounts** | Accounts, their ship-from addresses and logins |
| **Marketplaces** | Your connected webstores |
| **Customer details** | Customer addresses and contact information |
| **Catalog setup** | SKUs, label formats and printing templates |
| **Order log history** | Tracking and change history in the [shipping log](/docs/shipments/shipping-log) |
| **Automation rules** | Your [automation rules](/docs/automation) |
| **PrintWise** | Your [print jobs](/docs/printing/print-jobs) |

## What this means for you

- Search, filters, reports and exports only ever include your organization's orders.
- If you open a link to an order, label or setting that belongs to another organization, ShipNest won't show it.
- New features are checked to make sure they keep the same separation.

<Callout>
  What you can do **inside** your own organization still depends on your role. See [User roles](/docs/getting-started/user-roles).
</Callout>
