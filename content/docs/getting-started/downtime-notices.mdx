---
title: Downtime notices
description: How ShipNest tells you about planned updates.
---

When the ShipNest team plans an update (for example a release or a fix), a **downtime notice** banner appears at the top of every ShipNest page before it starts. This replaces the WhatsApp messages that were used before.

## What the banner tells you

The banner shows when the update starts and how long ShipNest will be unavailable — for example:

> *ShipNest update today at 7:30 PM — unavailable for about 30 minutes (7:30 PM – 8:00 PM).*

Times are shown in **your browser's time zone**, so each user sees the local time for where they are.

## Hide and reopen the banner

<Steps>
<Step>

### Hide it

Click the **×** on the banner. It shrinks to a small reminder in the top-right corner (for example **Update 7:30 PM**).

</Step>
<Step>

### Show it again

Click the reminder to open the full banner again.

</Step>
</Steps>

## What to do when you see a notice

- Finish or pause large tasks — such as bulk shipping or long label batches — a few minutes before the update starts, so they aren't interrupted.
- After the update, carry on as normal. Any batch you need again is in [Label downloads](/docs/shipments/label-downloads).

<Callout type="warn" title="During the update">
  While the update is running, ShipNest itself is unavailable, so the banner can't be shown. A separate maintenance page is planned for that time.
</Callout>

  No downtime was scheduled while this guide was written, so the banner isn't pictured here.
</Callout>
