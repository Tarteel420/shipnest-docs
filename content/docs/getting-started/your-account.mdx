## Your account panel

Click your **avatar** (top right) to open the account panel.

<Screenshot src="/images/getting-started/account-drawer.png" alt="Account panel with Release notes and Logout" caption="The account panel." />

From here you can:

- See the email address you are signed in with (click the copy icon to copy it).
- Open **Release notes** to see what's new in ShipNest.
- **Logout**.

## Admin menu

The **Admin** section of the sidebar contains:

| Page | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| **User Management** | Manage the users in your organization. Requires permission to manage users. |
| **Organization** | Organization-level settings. Requires permission to view organization settings. |
| **My Account** | Your account page. |

<Callout type="warn" title="Not documented yet">
  These pages are only available to users with admin permissions, so they are not covered in detail here yet. See [User roles](/docs/getting-started/user-roles).
</Callout>
