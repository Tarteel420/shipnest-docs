ShipNest runs in your web browser. After you sign in, you land on the **Shipments** page — the main working area.

<Screenshot src="/images/shipments/shipments-ready-to-ship.png" alt="ShipNest Shipments page with the left sidebar" caption="The Shipments page with the left sidebar." />

## The left sidebar

| Menu | What you do there |
| --- | --- |
| **Shipments** | See, filter and ship all your orders. See [Shipments Overview](/docs/shipments). |
| **Configurations** | Connect [carriers](/docs/configuration/carriers) and [webstores](/docs/configuration/webstores), set the [printing template](/docs/configuration/printing-template), and manage [custom packages](/docs/configuration/custom-packages), [automation rules](/docs/automation) and [tags](/docs/configuration/tags). |
| **Account settings** | Account-wide behaviour: [Shipping & rates](/docs/account-settings/shipping-and-rates), [Address validation](/docs/account-settings/address-validation), [Picking & fulfillment](/docs/account-settings/picking-and-fulfillment) and [Keyboard shortcuts](/docs/account-settings/keyboard-shortcuts). |
| **Printing** | PrintWise cloud printing: [Printers](/docs/printing/printers), [Print Jobs](/docs/printing/print-jobs) and [Configure Agent](/docs/printing/set-up-printwise-agent). |
| **Scan Labels** | Scan tracking numbers to confirm packages before pickup. See [Scan Labels](/docs/scan-labels). |
| **Reports** | Order, product, business and shipment reporting. See [Reports](/docs/reports). |
| **Admin** | User Management, Organization and My Account. See [Your account](/docs/getting-started/your-account). |

<Callout title="Menus depend on your role">
  Some items only appear, or only open, for users with the right permission. If a page says **You don't have permission** or **You don't have access to this page**, ask your organization admin. See [User roles](/docs/getting-started/user-roles).
</Callout>

Click the **‹** arrow at the top of the sidebar to collapse it and give the page more room.

## The header

- **Back arrow and page title** — returns you to the previous page.
- **Printer status (PDF ● / ZPL ●)** — shows the status of your default PDF and ZPL printers when PrintWise is used. It changes to a red **Printing Offline** chip when the printing agent is offline. See [Printing](/docs/printing).
- **Label downloads** (download arrow) — lists your label and packing-slip batches from the last 7 days so you can download them again. See [Label downloads](/docs/shipments/label-downloads).
- **Your avatar** — opens your account panel with **Release notes** and **Logout**.

<Screenshot src="/images/shipments/header-label-downloads-button.png" alt="Header: printer status, Label downloads, settings and avatar" />

## Downtime notices

Before a planned ShipNest update, a banner appears at the top of every page with the start time and how long ShipNest will be unavailable. See [Downtime notices](/docs/getting-started/downtime-notices).

## Help button

The green **?** button in the bottom-right corner opens in-app help. It may show a **NEW** badge when there is something new to look at.
