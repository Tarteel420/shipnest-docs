title: BTO sync

If your organization uses **BTO**, ShipNest sends your orders to BTO. The **BTO Sync** column on the [Shipments](/docs/shipments) page shows each order's sync status.

<Callout>
  The BTO Sync column and the retry option are only shown to organizations that use BTO. You can hide the column in [Shipping & rates](/docs/account-settings/shipping-and-rates#bto-sync).
</Callout>

## Automatic retries

Sometimes an order can't reach BTO — for example while BTO is down. ShipNest now retries these orders **automatically in the background**. When BTO is available again, the orders sync without anyone having to do anything.

## Retry an order yourself

You can also retry the sync for an order straight away:

<Steps>
<Step>

### Select the order

On the Shipments page, tick the order (or orders) that didn't sync.

</Step>
<Step>

### Retry

Choose **Retry BTO sync**. ShipNest sends the order to BTO again and updates the **BTO Sync** column.

</Step>
</Steps>
