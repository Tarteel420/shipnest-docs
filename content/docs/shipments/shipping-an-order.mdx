---
title: Ship an order
description: Create a shipping label and tracking number for an order.
---

Before you start, make sure you have connected a [carrier account](/docs/configuration/carriers) and a [webstore](/docs/configuration/webstores), and set up [printing](/docs/configuration/printing-template).

## Ship from the Order details page

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open the order

On [Shipments](/docs/shipments) → **Ready to Ship**, click the order's date / number to open [Order details](/docs/shipments/order-details).

</Step>
<Step>

### Check the addresses

Confirm the **Ship from** address and the customer's **Ship to** address. Fix anything flagged in red.

</Step>
<Step>

### Check the package

Enter or confirm the **dimensions**, **Weight** and **Package type**. Add a **Declared value**, **Reference** or **Invoice number** if needed, and set **Signature confirmation** and **Ship date**. See [Packages and items](/docs/shipments/packages-and-items).

</Step>
<Step>

### Choose a rate

In the rates table, select the service you want. Click the refresh icon to fetch rates again after changing the package. See [Rates and services](/docs/shipments/rates-and-services).

<Screenshot src="/images/shipments/order-details-rates.png" alt="Rates table" caption="Select a rate. A Late chip means the estimated delivery is after the ship-by / delivery date." />

</Step>
<Step>

### Ship it

Click **Update and ship**. For Shopify orders you can instead click **Ship with Shopify**, which buys the label and fulfils the order through Shopify.

</Step>
</Steps>

## What happens next

- ShipNest creates the shipment with the carrier and receives a **label** and **tracking number**.
- The label is printed or downloaded according to your [Printing template](/docs/configuration/printing-template) (PrintWise, desktop printing, or **Only Download PDF**).
- The order moves to **Shipped** and **To Process**. See [Dashboard tabs](/docs/shipments/dashboard-tabs).
- The action is recorded in the [Shipping log](/docs/shipments/shipping-log).

<Callout type="warn" title="Update and ship is greyed out?">
  A red ⚠ appears next to the button while something required is missing — for example a name, address, ZIP code, or a weight of 0. Fill in the fields marked in red and the button becomes available.
</Callout>

## Ship from the order list

Rows in **Ready to Ship** also have a **Ship** button. The row shows the package, weight and courier service that will be used, so check them before you click it.

To ship many orders at once, see [Bulk actions](/docs/shipments/bulk-actions).
