title: Dashboard tabs (order statuses)
description: What each Shipments tab means and when an order appears there.
---

The tabs across the top of the order table group orders by status. The number on each tab is the count of orders in it.

| Tab | What it contains |
| --- | --- |
| **Ready to Ship** | Orders synced from your webstores (or created manually) that are ready to be shipped. |
| **Needs Attention** | Orders with a problem that must be fixed before shipping. See [Fixing Needs Attention orders](/docs/shipments/needs-attention). |
| **Partially Shipped** | Orders with more than one shipment where some, but not all, have been shipped. |
| **Shipped** | Orders with a created shipment, label and tracking number. |
| **To Process** | Shipped orders whose labels have not yet been scanned on [Scan Labels](/docs/scan-labels) or picked up by the carrier. |
| **Cancelled** | Cancelled orders. |
| **Returned** | Orders with a return. See [Returns](/docs/shipments/returns). |
| **All** | Every order, with an extra **Status** column and **Tracking** column. |

## How orders move between tabs

- A new order starts in **Ready to Ship**, or in **Needs Attention** if something is missing or invalid.
- When you ship it, it moves to **Shipped** and also appears in **To Process**.
- Scanning the label on [Scan Labels](/docs/scan-labels), or the carrier confirming pickup, removes it from **To Process**. It stays in **Shipped**.
- Orders with several shipments stay in **Partially Shipped** until every shipment is shipped.

## Tracking status

In the **Tracking** column (Shipped, To Process and All tabs) each shipment shows its tracking number, a **Status** (for example *Label Created*) and the **Shipped on** date. Orders shipped through Shopify Shipping show **Shipped using Shopify**.

Tracking statuses you can filter on: **Delivered**, **In Transit**, **Picked Up**, **Processed** and **Label Created**.

## Show, hide or reorder tabs

You can hide tabs you don't use and change their order. See [Customize the dashboard](/docs/shipments/customize-dashboard#customize-tabs).
