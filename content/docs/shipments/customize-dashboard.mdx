## Customize tabs

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open Customize tabs

On the Shipments page, click **Customize tabs** (right of the tabs).

</Step>
<Step>

### Choose and order tabs

In **Selected**, untick a tab to hide it, or drag the handle to change the order (top to bottom = left to right). Hidden tabs move to **Unselected** — tick one to show it again.

</Step>
<Step>

### Save

Click **Save**. Use **Reset to default** to restore the original tabs.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/shipments/ship-customize-tabs.png" alt="Dashboard Tabs dialog" caption="Tabs with a lock icon are compulsory and cannot be removed." />

**Ready to Ship**, **Shipped**, **Cancelled**, **Returned** and **All** are compulsory. **Needs Attention**, **Partially Shipped** and **To Process** can be hidden. Counts are not calculated for hidden tabs.

## Customize columns

Click the **columns** icon at the right end of the table header to choose which columns appear: BTO Sync, Ship from, Value, Courier service, Paid, Ship to, Items, Dimensions, Shipping — and which row actions are shown (Ship, Slip, Clone, Cancel).

## Sort the table

Click a column heading (Order, Ship to, Items, Value, Courier service, Shipping, Paid) to sort by it.

## Paging

Use **Rows per page** and **Jump to page** at the bottom of the table. The page number and the orders shown always stay in step, even when you click through pages quickly.
