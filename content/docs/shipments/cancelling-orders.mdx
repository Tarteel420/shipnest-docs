---
title: Cancelling orders
description: Cancel an order that should not be shipped.
---

Use **Cancel** when an order should not be shipped at all.

## Cancel an order

<Steps>
<Step>

### Click Cancel

- From the list: click **Cancel** on the order's row (Ready to Ship or Needs Attention).
- From [Order details](/docs/shipments/order-details): click **Cancel** in the header.

</Step>
<Step>

### Check the order

ShipNest asks **Cancel this order?** and shows the order so you can make sure it's the right one: the store, order number, order date, **Ship to** customer, **Value**, what was **Paid** for shipping, and the **Items**.

<Screenshot src="/images/shipments/cancel-order-confirm.png" alt="Cancel this order? confirmation" caption="Check the order number and customer before you confirm." />

</Step>
<Step>

### Confirm

Click **Cancel order** to cancel it, or **Keep order** (or **×**) to go back without changing anything.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warn">
  Cancelling may not be reversible. Always check the order number in the confirmation before you click **Cancel order**.
</Callout>

Cancelled orders move to the **Cancelled** tab. Orders cancelled on the marketplace also appear there after the next [sync](/docs/shipments/syncing-orders).

<Callout type="warn" title="Already shipped?">
  If a label has already been created, [void the label](/docs/shipments/shipped-orders#void-a-label) instead — that cancels the label with the carrier.
</Callout>
