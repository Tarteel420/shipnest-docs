## Choose marketplaces

The **Marketplaces** selector at the top of the page decides which webstores' orders are shown.

<Screenshot src="/images/shipments/ship-marketplaces.png" alt="Marketplaces selector open" caption="Tick All marketplaces or the individual stores you want to see." />

**ShipNest** in this list means orders created manually with [New shipment](/docs/shipments/new-shipment).

## Search

Type in **Search shipments** to find an order.

## Use the Filters panel

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open Filters

Click **Filters**. The number on the button shows how many filters are active.

</Step>
<Step>

### Pick your filters

Choose values in any section. The list updates as you go.

</Step>
<Step>

### Check the chips

Active filters appear as chips next to the filter-set menu. When there isn't room for every chip, a **View more (+n)** button shows how many are hidden — click it to show them all. Click **×** on a chip to remove it, or **Clear** to remove all.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/shipments/ship-filters-panel.png" alt="Filters panel" caption="The Filters panel, with saved filter sets at the top." />

### Available filters

| Filter | Options |
| --- | --- |
| **Delivery speed** | Expedited, Standard. *Expedited* means the customer paid for faster delivery. |
| **Fulfilled by** | ShipNest, 3rd Party |
| **Service** | Carrier services (search, or **Show all services**) |
| **Weight (lbs)** | From – To |
| **Package type** | Carrier boxes, Box/Other and your custom packages |
| **Notes** | Orders with notes |
| **Channel** | Sales channel |
| **Destination state** | Based on the customer's destination address |
| **Price** | From – To |
| **Tracking status** | Delivered, In Transit, Picked Up, Processed, Label Created |
| **P.O. Box** | P.O. box orders only |
| **Tags** | Any of your [tags](/docs/configuration/tags) |
| **Multi-shipment orders** | Orders with multiple shipments |
| **Date** | Custom Range, Today, This Week, Last Week, Last 7 Days, Current Month |
| **Marketplace** | Your connected stores and ShipNest |
| **Multi-package orders** | Exact, 2 or more … 5 or more |
| **Ship-by** | Overdue, Today, Tomorrow, This week |

## Reorder or pin filters

Click the **gear** icon at the top of the Filters panel (**Filter settings**). Drag filters to reorder them and pin the ones you use most to the top. **Reset to default** restores the original order. Click **Done**.

## Save your filters

To reuse a combination, click **Save as filter set** at the bottom of the panel. See [Filter sets](/docs/shipments/filter-sets).
