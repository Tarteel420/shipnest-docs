## Packing slips for many orders (Slip Preview)

<Steps>
<Step>

### Click Slip Preview

On the [Shipments](/docs/shipments) page, click **Slip Preview (n)**. The number is how many orders are included.

</Step>
<Step>

### Wait for generation

The **Generating packing slips** window shows progress — how many were generated and how many failed.

<Screenshot src="/images/shipments/slip-preview-dialog.png" alt="Generating packing slips window" caption="Use the All / Generated / Failed tabs and the search box to review results." />

</Step>
<Step>

### Open the slips

The slips open in a new browser tab as one PDF. If your browser blocks the new tab, the window tells you — allow pop-ups for ShipNest and try again.

</Step>
<Step>

### Check failures

Open the **Failed** tab to see which orders failed and the **Error**. Use **Grid order** to change the order of the list. Click **Close** when done.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout>
  Each Slip Preview run is saved for 7 days. If the PDF didn't open or the download failed, download it again from [Label downloads](/docs/shipments/label-downloads). While a batch is downloading, the button shows a loader and is disabled until it finishes.
</Callout>

## Packing slip for one order

On a row, click **Slip** to print the slip, or **▾ → Download** to download it. The same **Slip** button is on the [Order details](/docs/shipments/order-details) page.

To include Shopify order notes on slips, see [Picking & fulfillment](/docs/account-settings/picking-and-fulfillment).

## Download labels

Select orders and click **Labels (PDF)** to download their labels in one file.

To reprint the label of one shipped order, use **⋮ → PDF** on its row. See [Shipped orders](/docs/shipments/shipped-orders).

## Export orders

Click **▾** next to **Labels (PDF)** and choose:

- **Orders in CSV**
- **Orders in Excel**

Use exports for reporting, reconciliation or sharing with your team. For charts and totals, see [Reports](/docs/reports).
