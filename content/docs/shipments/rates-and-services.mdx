ShipNest fetches live rates from your [carrier accounts](/docs/configuration/carriers) so you can compare price and delivery speed.

## The rates table

On [Order details](/docs/shipments/order-details), the rates table lists one row per available service:

| Column | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Account** | The carrier account the rate comes from. A red **Late** chip means the service is not expected to arrive in time. |
| **Service** | The carrier and service, for example *FedEx 2Day® (One Rate)*. |
| **Transit** | The estimated delivery date. |
| **Rate** | The price. |

Select the radio button of the service you want. Click **Show More** to see more services.

## Refresh rates

Rates depend on the package. After changing dimensions, weight, package type or address, click the **refresh** icon at the top of the rates table to fetch rates again.

<Callout title="FedEx address checks">
  If FedEx can't validate the address, you still get FedEx rates — a failed address check no longer blocks all FedEx rates.
</Callout>

<Callout title="Rates need a weight">
  Rates can't be fetched while the package weight is 0 or empty.
</Callout>

## Let automation choose for you

Instead of choosing a service manually, you can let rules do it:

- [Select shipping service](/docs/automation/select-shipping-service) — always use a specific account or service for matching orders.
- [Fetch Rates](/docs/automation/fetch-rates) — fetch rates and pick the cheapest on-time service automatically.
- [Fallback Selection](/docs/automation/fallback-selection) — correct orders left on an unwanted or too-expensive service.

You can also fetch rates for many selected orders with the **Bulk fetch rates** [keyboard shortcut](/docs/account-settings/keyboard-shortcuts).
