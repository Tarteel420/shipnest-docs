---
title: Create a shipment manually
description: Ship something that didn't come from a connected webstore.
---

Use **New shipment** to create an order by hand — for example a replacement, a sample, or an order taken by phone. Manually created orders show the marketplace **ShipNest**.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Click New shipment

On the [Shipments](/docs/shipments) page, click **New shipment**. A **New Shipment** page opens with an automatic reference such as *SN-…*.

<Screenshot src="/images/shipments/new-shipment-form.png" alt="New Shipment page" caption="Fields marked in red are required." />

</Step>
<Step>

### Add the items

Click **Add items**. In **Edit items**, click **+ Add item** and enter the **Item title**, **SKU**, **Quantity** and **Unit price**. Repeat for each item, then click **Save and Close**.

<Screenshot src="/images/shipments/new-shipment-add-item.png" alt="Edit items dialog" />

</Step>
<Step>

### Enter the addresses

Choose the **Ship from** address and enter the customer's **Ship to** details: first name, address, city, state, ZIP code and phone are required.

</Step>
<Step>

### Enter the package

Enter dimensions and a **Weight** (it can't be 0), and choose a package type. See [Packages and items](/docs/shipments/packages-and-items).

</Step>
<Step>

### Choose a rate and ship

Select a rate, then click **Create and ship**. The button stays disabled (with a red ⚠) until all required fields are valid.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Create a stand-alone return

Click the **▾** next to **New shipment** and choose **Return shipment**. This opens a blank return where **Ship to** is your address and **Ship from** is the customer. See [Returns](/docs/shipments/returns).

<Callout type="warn">
  Opening **New shipment** creates a draft straight away, even before you click **Create and ship**.
</Callout>
