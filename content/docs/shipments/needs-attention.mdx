title: Fixing Needs Attention orders
description: Why orders end up in Needs Attention and how to fix them.
---

Orders that can't be shipped yet are moved to the **Needs Attention** tab.

<Screenshot src="/images/shipments/ship-needs-attention.png" alt="Needs Attention tab" caption="A red ⚠ under the date means the order has a problem. Ship and Clone are disabled." />

## Why an order is here

These always send an order to Needs Attention:

- An **invalid address**
- A **P.O. Box** address
- A **Shopify fulfillment hold**

Your admin can also choose extra criteria in [Shipping & rates](/docs/account-settings/shipping-and-rates#needs-attention-criteria): **Missing weight**, **Missing dimensions**, **Order value over threshold** and **Missing shipping address**.

## See the exact problem

Hover the red **⚠** under the order date. Examples of messages:

- *First name is required to ship this order.*
- *Please enter package weight*
- *No shipping rate is available for this shipment. Please fetch rates and select a service.*

## Fix it

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open the order

Click the order date / number to open [Order details](/docs/shipments/order-details).

</Step>
<Step>

### Correct the details

Fill in the missing field (for example the name, address or package weight) and choose a rate. See [Rates and services](/docs/shipments/rates-and-services).

</Step>
<Step>

### Ship

Click **Update and ship**.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout>
  You can also use an [Order validation](/docs/automation/order-validation) rule to tag or pause orders that need a person to look at them.
</Callout>
