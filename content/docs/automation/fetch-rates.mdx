---
title: Fetch Rates
description: Fetch carrier rates automatically and optionally pick the cheapest on-time service.
---

*Fetches carrier rates for eligible shipments (package weight must be greater than zero). With "Select cheapest & on-time" enabled the cheapest on-time service is chosen automatically; disable it to fetch rates without selecting a service.*

<Screenshot src="/images/automation/ar-fetch-rates.png" alt="Fetch Rates rule" />

## Create the rule

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open and name

Click **Fetch Rates**, enter a **Rule name** and optional **Description**, and set **Test mode** if needed.

</Step>
<Step>

### Choose the action mode

Under **Action**, leave **Select cheapest & on-time service** on to choose the service automatically. Turn it off to fetch and store rates without selecting one — *When off, rates are fetched and stored but no shipping service is selected.*

</Step>
<Step>

### Filter (optional)

*Limit the rule to specific marketplaces or order tags. Leave empty to apply to every eligible order.*

</Step>
<Step>

### Save

Check **Review and save** and click **Create rule**.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout>
  Only one Fetch Rates rule is allowed per account. If the template button is greyed out, edit the existing rule from the Rules list.
</Callout>

**Tip:** Turn **Select cheapest & on-time service** off if you want to make the final choice with a [Fallback Selection](/docs/automation/fallback-selection) rule.
