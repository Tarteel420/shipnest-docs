title: Fallback Selection
description: Correct orders left on an unwanted service or too high a rate.
---

> *Fixes orders left too expensive or on the wrong service. Can skip services when re-picking.*

*Runs last, after rates have been fetched. Match the orders your earlier rules left on an unwanted service or too high a rate, then re-do the selection — a different package, a different carrier, and/or a fresh rate fetch that skips services you exclude.*

<Screenshot src="/images/automation/ar-fallback-top.png" alt="Fallback Selection — top of panel" />

## Create the rule

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open and name

Click **Fallback Selection**, enter a **Rule name**, optional **Description** and **Test mode** if needed.

</Step>
<Step>

### Set the filter

Match on what earlier rules produced — tags, courier service and shipping cost. For example *cost is greater than 20* or *courier service is Ground Economy*.

</Step>
<Step>

### Package (optional)

Turn on **Apply a different package** to swap the package.

</Step>
<Step>

### Carrier (optional)

Choose a **Shipping account** and/or **Courier service**, or leave both empty to let the rate fetch decide.

</Step>
<Step>

### Rates (optional)

Keep **Re-fetch rates** and **Select cheapest & on-time service** on (recommended). Use **Never auto-select these services** to skip services when picking — *excluded services are still rated and stay visible on the shipment, they are just skipped when picking automatically, so the next-cheapest service wins.*

<Screenshot src="/images/automation/ar-fallback-rates.png" alt="Carrier, Rates and Behaviour sections" />

</Step>
<Step>

### Behaviour

Keep both boxes ticked (recommended):

- **Apply only once per order** — otherwise an order still above your cost threshold would be re-processed and re-rated on every run.
- **Clear the previous rate and service first** — otherwise a re-fetch keeps the service the order already has.

</Step>
<Step>

### Reselect courier service (optional)

**Re-pick the courier service** runs on its own: rates are re-fetched and the cheapest matching rate is selected, while the package, carrier and exclusion steps are left alone.

</Step>
<Step>

### Save

Review the chips (for example *Runs after Fetch Rates*, *Re-fetches rates*, *Once per order*) and click **Create rule**.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout>
  Use this together with [Fetch Rates](/docs/automation/fetch-rates): Fetch Rates gets the rates, Fallback Selection corrects the orders that ended up on the wrong service or too high a rate.
</Callout>
