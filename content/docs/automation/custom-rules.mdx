---
title: Custom rules (+ New Rule)
description: Build your own logic when no template fits.
---

Click the green **+ New Rule** button (top right of the Automation Rules page) to build a rule from scratch. It gives you the widest choice of fields and actions.

## What's different

- **101 fields** across BTO kit, order item, shipping account, market, package, ship-to address, order and shipment. See [Fields and operators](/docs/automation/fields-and-operators).
- **21 actions**, including setting package dimensions, shipping account, courier service, ship-from address, holds and tags. See [Actions reference](/docs/automation/actions-reference).
- **Stop after match** — stop checking further branches once one matches.
- **Else-if branches** and an **Else (otherwise)** fallback.

## Create a custom rule

<Steps>
<Step>

### Click + New Rule

The **New Rule** panel opens.

</Step>
<Step>

### Name and options

Enter a **Rule name**, and optionally switch on **Test mode** and/or **Stop after match**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Set the first condition

Add filters under **Filter Conditions**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Add actions

Click **Add Action**, choose the action and its settings, and confirm with **Add Action**. Add as many actions as you need.

</Step>
<Step>

### Add branches (optional)

Click **Add else-if branch** for more filter + action pairs, and enable **Else (otherwise)** for orders that match none.

</Step>
<Step>

### Save

Click **Save Rule**.

</Step>
</Steps>
