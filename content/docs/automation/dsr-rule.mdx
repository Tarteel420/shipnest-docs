---
title: DSR rule (signature requirement)
description: Set the delivery signature requirement automatically.
---

> *Sets what an order needs before it can ship.*

The **DSR rule** sets the **signature confirmation** on matching orders.

## Create a DSR rule

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open and name

Click **DSR rule**, enter a **Rule name**, optional **Description**, and **Test mode** if needed.

</Step>
<Step>

### Add conditions

Under **Filter Conditions** add the conditions. The DSR rule offers four fields: **Market Name**, **Tags**, **Order Total** and **Requested Shipping Method**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Choose the signature

Click **Add Action** → **Set Signature Confirmation**, then choose the **Signature confirmation**: *Default Confirmation*, *No Signature*, *Direct Signature* or *Adult Signature*. Click **Add Action**.

<Screenshot src="/images/automation/ar-dsr-action.png" alt="Set Signature Confirmation action" />

</Step>
<Step>

### Add branches (optional)

Click **Add else-if branch** for other scenarios, and enable **Else (otherwise)** for a default.

</Step>
<Step>

### Save

Click **Save Rule**.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Example

**Adult signature on high-value orders** — Filter: **Order Total** `>` 1000 → **Set Signature Confirmation**: *Adult Signature*.

<Callout>
  For eBay orders there is also a built-in setting: **Require signature on high-value eBay orders** in [Shipping & rates](/docs/account-settings/shipping-and-rates).
</Callout>
