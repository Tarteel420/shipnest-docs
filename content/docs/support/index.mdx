## Contact Support

ShipNest Support accepts requests by **email** and **WhatsApp**. Every request is logged and tracked until it is resolved.

<Callout type="warn" title="Contact details">
  Use the support email address and WhatsApp number provided to your organization by 99 Technologies.
</Callout>

## What to include

To get a fast answer, include:

- What you were trying to do (for example *ship order 1563*).
- What happened instead, including any error message.
- The date and time it happened.
- The order or tracking number.
- Screenshots of the screen.

## Help inside ShipNest

- The green **?** button (bottom-right of every page) opens in-app help.
- **Release notes** in your account panel list what's new. See [Your account](/docs/getting-started/your-account).
