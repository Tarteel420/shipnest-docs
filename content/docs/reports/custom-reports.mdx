title: Custom reports
description: Build and save your own reports.
---

Custom reports let you choose exactly what to measure and how to show it. **Saved reports are shared with everyone in your organisation.**

## Build a report

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open the builder

On the **Custom reports** tab, click **+ New report** (or **Build a report**).

</Step>
<Step>

### Name it

Enter a **Report name**, for example *Top products by revenue*.

</Step>
<Step>

### Group by

Choose how to split the data: **No grouping (totals)**, **Product**, **Marketplace**, **Category**, **Region**, **City**, **Carrier** or **Month**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Pick measures

Tick one or more: Order count, Order value, Shipping cost, Avg order value, Margin (value − shipping), Shipping cost / order, Gift box value, On-time rate, Shipped labels, Voided labels, Shipments.

</Step>
<Step>

### Sort and display

Choose **Sort by** (one of your measures) and **Display as**: **Table**, **Bar chart**, **Line chart**, **Pie chart** or **Donut chart**. Check the **Preview**.

<Screenshot src="/images/reports/reports-build-report.png" alt="Build a report dialog" />

</Step>
<Step>

### Save

Click **Save report**.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Manage saved reports

<Screenshot src="/images/reports/reports-custom-saved.png" alt="Saved custom report" />

Each saved report shows its measures, grouping and who created it, with **View**, **Edit**, **Duplicate** and **delete** (trash) actions.
