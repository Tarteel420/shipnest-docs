title: Business tab
description: Revenue trends, seasonality and where your customers are.
---

<Screenshot src="/images/reports/reports-business.png" alt="Reports Business tab" />

| Report | Shows | Options |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Revenue trend** | Order value by marketplace over time. | Chart type, date range, **Compare**, **Data labels** |
| **Seasonal variance** | Demand by product, month over month. | Heatmap, date range, **Compare** |
| **Customer distribution** | Where orders are shipping to — share, order count and order value. | **Region** or **City** |

Drag the **⠿** handle on a report to reorder it.
