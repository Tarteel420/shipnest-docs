## Widgets

The KPI tiles at the top (Total orders, Total order value, Average order value, Gift box value) show the change versus the previous period.

- **Add widget** — add *Shipping cost*, *Margin (value − shipping)*, *Shipping cost / order*, *Shipped labels* or *Voided labels*.
- **Gear** on a tile — configure it. **×** removes it.
- Drag the **⠿** handle to reorder.

## Top Products

| Control | Use |
| --- | --- |
| **Chart type** | Bar, Line, Area, Pie, Donut, Stacked bar, Heatmap (heatmap needs the Marketplace column). |
| **Date** | Its own date range. |
| **Search products** | Find a product. |
| **Columns** | Product, Marketplace, Order count, Product revenue, % of product revenue, Data point labels. |

The table lists each product with **Order count**, **Product revenue** and **% of product revenue**.

**Gift box value** is reported separately and excluded from the product ranking.
