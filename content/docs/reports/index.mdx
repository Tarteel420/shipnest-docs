Click **Reports** in the sidebar. Reports are split into five tabs: [Overview](/docs/reports/overview-tab), [Product](/docs/reports/product-tab), [Business](/docs/reports/business-tab), [Shipment](/docs/reports/shipment-tab) and [Custom reports](/docs/reports/custom-reports).

## Filters at the top

These apply to every tab:

| Filter | Options |
| --- | --- |
| **Date** | Last 7 days, Last 14 days, Last 30 days, Last 90 days, Last month, Year to date, Last year, or a **Custom range** (From / To → **Apply**). |
| **Marketplace** | **All marketplaces** or one marketplace. |
| **Carrier** | **All carriers** or one carrier account. |

## Notes about the figures

Under some charts, click **n notes about these figures** to see how they're calculated. In short:

- **Product revenue** is item-level — it excludes tax, shipping and order-level discounts.
- Per-product order counts don't add up to total orders — an order with two products counts once for each.
- Shipping cost is 0 until a label is bought, so it under-reports unshipped orders in the period.
- Totals don't convert currencies.
