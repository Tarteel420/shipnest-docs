title: Printers

Go to **Printing → Printers**.

<Screenshot src="/images/printing/printing-printers.png" alt="Printers page" />

The page lists every printer enabled on your [PrintWise agents](/docs/printing/set-up-printwise-agent), split into **PDF Printers** and **ZPL Printers**. Each card shows the printer, the agent computer, and its **Status** (for example *Agent Offline*). Turn on **Show available only** to hide printers that can't print right now.

## Set a default printer

Click **Set as Default** on a printer card. Your defaults appear at the top under **Default Printers**. Set one default PDF printer and one default ZPL printer.

To remove a default, click **Clear Default**.

## Change a printer's type

Click the **gear** (Configure printer) on its card and choose:

- **Document Printer** — for PDF, Word, images and other documents.
- **ZPL Label Printer** — for Zebra ZPL label printing (thermal printers).

Click **Save**.

<Screenshot src="/images/printing/printing-configure-printer.png" alt="Configure Printer dialog" />

## Remove a printer

Click the red **trash** icon on its card.
