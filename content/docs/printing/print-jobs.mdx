Go to **Printing → Print Jobs**.

<Screenshot src="/images/printing/printing-jobs.png" alt="Print Jobs page" />

## Find a job

- **Search by** — **Order Number** or **Tracking Number**, then type in the search box.
- **Scope** — **My Jobs** or **All Organization Jobs**.
- **Status tabs** — **All**, **Pending**, **Printing**, **Completed**, **Failed**, **Cancelled** (with counts).

Each row shows the **Shipment** (order number, tracking number and store), **Label Type**, **Printer**, **Status** and **Created** time. Use **download** to get the label or the **eye** to open the job.

## Download several labels as one PDF

Tick jobs (you can select across pages) and click **Download Bulk PDF**.

## Job details

Click the **eye** icon to open **Job Details**: the status (with the reason if it failed), **File Information** with **Download PDF**, the **Printer** and agent, and a **Timeline** (Created, Failed / Completed…).

<Screenshot src="/images/printing/printing-job-details.png" alt="Job Details for a failed job" caption="A failed job explains what went wrong and what to do." />

## Printing errors stay until fixed

A failed job keeps its error until the cause is actually resolved — errors can't be cleared without fixing the problem. Once the cause is fixed and the label is reprinted, the error clears through the normal flow.

## Reprint a failed job

Fix the cause (for example start the PrintWise agent on the printing computer), then reprint the label from the shipment — **⋮ → PDF** on the shipped order. See [Shipped orders](/docs/shipments/shipped-orders#reprint-a-label).
