---
title: Connect USPS
description: Add a USPS account to ShipNest.
---

USPS needs the same details as UPS and FedEx, plus three USPS-specific IDs.

## What you need

- Your USPS **account number**, **Client ID** and **Client secret**.
- Your **Mailer ID**, **Manifest Mailer ID** and **Payment account number**.
- The ship-from address.

## Add the account

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open the USPS form

Go to **Configurations → Carrier settings**, click **+ New carrier**, then click **USPS**.

<Screenshot src="/images/configuration/carrier-usps-form.png" alt="USPS account configuration form" />

</Step>
<Step>

### Enter the account details

| Field | Enter |
| --- | --- |
| **Account number** \* | Your USPS account number. |
| **Client id** \* | Your USPS API client ID. |
| **Client secret** | Your USPS API client secret. |
| **Mailer ID** \* | Your USPS Mailer ID. |
| **Manifest mailer id** \* | Your USPS manifest Mailer ID. |
| **Payment account number** \* | The USPS account labels are paid from. |
| **Default Package Type** | The package type to use by default with this account. |

</Step>
<Step>

### Enter the shipping address

Fill in the ship-from address (Company, First name, Address 1, City, State, Zip code, Email and Phone number are required).

</Step>
<Step>

### Authenticate

Click **Authenticate**, then [switch the account on](/docs/configuration/webstores/managing-webstores#choose-carrier-accounts-for-a-webstore) for your webstores.

</Step>
</Steps>
