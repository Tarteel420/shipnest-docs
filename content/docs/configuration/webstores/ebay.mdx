---
title: Connect eBay
description: Connect an eBay seller account by signing in to eBay.
---

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open the eBay form

Go to **Configurations → Webstore settings**, click **+ New webstore**, then click **eBay**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Connect to eBay

Click **Connect to eBay**. Sign in to eBay and approve access. You are returned to ShipNest and the webstore is added.

</Step>
<Step>

### Choose carrier accounts

[Switch on the carrier accounts](/docs/configuration/webstores/managing-webstores#choose-carrier-accounts-for-a-webstore) for this webstore.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout>
  To require a signature on high-value eBay orders automatically, see [Shipping & rates](/docs/account-settings/shipping-and-rates#require-signature-on-high-value-ebay-orders).
</Callout>
