---
title: Connect Newegg and Newegg Business
description: Connect Newegg or Newegg Business with API credentials.
---

Newegg and Newegg Business are separate options in the **Webstore configuration** panel. The only difference is the last field.

<Tabs items={['Newegg', 'Newegg Business']}>
<Tab value="Newegg">

| Field | Enter |
| --- | --- |
| **Web store name** \* | A name to recognise this store. |
| **Seller id** \* | Your Newegg seller ID. |
| **Secret key** \* | Your Newegg secret key. |
| **Authentication code** \* | Your Newegg authentication code. |

</Tab>
<Tab value="Newegg Business">

| Field | Enter |
| --- | --- |
| **Web store name** \* | A name to recognise this store. |
| **Seller ID** \* | Your Newegg Business seller ID. |
| **Secret Key** \* | Your secret key. |
| **API Key** \* | Your Newegg Business API key. |

</Tab>
</Tabs>

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open the form

Go to **Configurations → Webstore settings**, click **+ New webstore**, then click **Newegg** or **Newegg Business**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Enter the details

Fill in the fields above using the credentials from the Newegg seller portal.

</Step>
<Step>

### Authenticate

Click **Authenticate**, then [switch on the carrier accounts](/docs/configuration/webstores/managing-webstores#choose-carrier-accounts-for-a-webstore) for the store.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Large order volumes

When Newegg asks ShipNest to slow down, ShipNest waits for a limited time and then continues, so a Newegg sync can't stay paused indefinitely. See [Syncing orders](/docs/shipments/syncing-orders#large-order-volumes-and-marketplace-limits).
