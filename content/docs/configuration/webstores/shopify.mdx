---
title: Connect Shopify
description: Connect a Shopify store using its store name and access token.
---

## What you need

- Your Shopify **store name** — for example `my-store` or `my-store.myshopify.com`.
- A Shopify **offline/admin access token** for this store, created in your Shopify admin.

## Connect the store

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open the Shopify form

Go to **Configurations → Webstore settings**, click **+ New webstore**, then click **Shopify**.

<Screenshot src="/images/configuration/webstore-shopify.png" alt="Shopify configuration form" />

</Step>
<Step>

### Enter the store name

In **Web store name**, enter the Shopify store name (for example *my-store* or *my-store.myshopify.com*).

</Step>
<Step>

### Enter the access token

In **Access token**, paste the Shopify offline/admin access token for this store.

</Step>
<Step>

### Authenticate

Click **Authenticate**. The store appears in your webstore list.

</Step>
<Step>

### Choose carrier accounts

[Switch on the carrier accounts](/docs/configuration/webstores/managing-webstores#choose-carrier-accounts-for-a-webstore) this store should use.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Shopify-only features

- **Ship with Shopify** and **Ship all with Shopify** buy labels and fulfil orders through Shopify in one step. They work only for Shopify orders — other orders show a message that they can't be shipped with Shopify. See [Bulk actions](/docs/shipments/bulk-actions#ship-all-with-shopify).
- Address warnings from Shopify are kept up to date after each sync.
- **Print Shopify order notes on packing slips** — see [Picking & fulfillment](/docs/account-settings/picking-and-fulfillment).
- Orders on a **Shopify fulfillment hold** always go to [Needs Attention](/docs/shipments/needs-attention).
