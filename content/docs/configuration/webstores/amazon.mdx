## Connect the account

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open the Amazon form

Go to **Configurations → Webstore settings**, click **+ New webstore**, then click **Amazon**.

<Screenshot src="/images/configuration/webstore-amazon.png" alt="Amazon configuration form" />

</Step>
<Step>

### Enter your company details

Fill in **Company**, **First name**, **Address 1**, **City**, **State** and **Zip code** (required), plus **Last name**, **Address 2**, **Email** and **Phone number** if you have them. **Country** is US.

</Step>
<Step>

### Connect to Amazon

Click **Connect to Amazon**. You are taken to Amazon to sign in and approve access, then returned to ShipNest.

</Step>
<Step>

### Choose carrier accounts

[Switch on the carrier accounts](/docs/configuration/webstores/managing-webstores#choose-carrier-accounts-for-a-webstore) for this webstore.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Large order volumes

If a lot of Amazon orders arrive at once, ShipNest pauses when Amazon's rate limit is reached and resumes where it stopped, instead of failing or starting again from the first page. See [Syncing orders](/docs/shipments/syncing-orders#large-order-volumes-and-marketplace-limits).
