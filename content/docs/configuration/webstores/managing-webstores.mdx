---
title: Managing webstores
description: Turn syncing on or off, edit credentials and choose carrier accounts.
---

## Turn a webstore on or off

On **Configurations → Webstore settings**, use the **Active** switch on the webstore's row. Only active webstores sync orders.

## Edit a webstore

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open it

Click **⋮ → Edit** on the webstore's row. The webstore page opens with two tabs.

</Step>
<Step>

### Account information

Update the name or credentials and click **Save changes**.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Choose carrier accounts for a webstore

Each webstore can use a different set of carrier accounts.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open the Carrier accounts tab

Click **⋮ → Edit** on the webstore, then open **Carrier accounts**.

<Screenshot src="/images/configuration/webstore-carrier-accounts.png" alt="Webstore Carrier accounts tab" />

</Step>
<Step>

### Switch accounts on or off

Use the **Active** switch next to each carrier. Only active carriers are used for this webstore's orders.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout>
  Carrier accounts are added under [Carrier settings](/docs/configuration/carriers). After adding a new carrier account, check this tab to make sure it is switched on for the webstores that should use it.
</Callout>
