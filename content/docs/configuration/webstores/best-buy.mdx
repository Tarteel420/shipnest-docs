---
title: Connect Best Buy
description: Connect a Best Buy marketplace account with an API key.
---

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open the Best Buy form

Go to **Configurations → Webstore settings**, click **+ New webstore**, then click **Best Buy**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Enter the details

| Field | Enter |
| --- | --- |
| **Web store name** \* | A name to recognise this store. |
| **API Key** \* | Your Best Buy marketplace API key. |

</Step>
<Step>

### Authenticate

Click **Authenticate**, then [switch on the carrier accounts](/docs/configuration/webstores/managing-webstores#choose-carrier-accounts-for-a-webstore) for the store.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout>
  Orders that you re-ship stay shipped — they no longer go back to Unshipped after the next sync.
</Callout>
