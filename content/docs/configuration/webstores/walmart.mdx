title: Connect Walmart
description: Connect a Walmart seller account with API credentials.
---

## What you need

Your Walmart **Seller ID** and **Secret key**, from Walmart's seller/developer portal.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open the Walmart form

Go to **Configurations → Webstore settings**, click **+ New webstore**, then click **Walmart**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Enter the details

| Field | Enter |
| --- | --- |
| **Web store name** \* | A name to recognise this store in ShipNest. |
| **Seller id** \* | Your Walmart seller ID. |
| **Secret key** \* | Your Walmart secret key. |

</Step>
<Step>

### Authenticate

Click **Authenticate**, then [switch on the carrier accounts](/docs/configuration/webstores/managing-webstores#choose-carrier-accounts-for-a-webstore) for the store.

</Step>
</Steps>

## How Walmart orders arrive

Walmart sends ShipNest a notification when an order is created or changes, so new Walmart orders appear quickly. ShipNest also checks Walmart regularly for anything a notification missed. See [Syncing orders](/docs/shipments/syncing-orders#how-new-orders-arrive).
