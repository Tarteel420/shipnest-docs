title: Connect Temu
description: Connect a Temu seller account to ShipNest.
---

Temu is now a supported marketplace. You can connect it by signing in to Temu, or by pasting a seller access token.

## Connect the store

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open the Temu form

Go to **Configurations → Webstore settings**, click **+ New webstore**, then click **Temu**.

<Screenshot src="/images/configuration/webstore-temu.png" alt="Temu configuration form" />

</Step>
<Step>

### Name the store

In **Web store name** (required), enter a name to recognise this store in ShipNest.

</Step>
<Step>

### Connect

Choose one:

- **Connect to Temu** — sign in to Temu and approve access. You are returned to ShipNest.
- **Access token (optional)** — *Paste a seller access token instead of Connect to Temu*, then click **Authenticate**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Choose carrier accounts

[Switch on the carrier accounts](/docs/configuration/webstores/managing-webstores#choose-carrier-accounts-for-a-webstore) this store should use.

</Step>
</Steps>
