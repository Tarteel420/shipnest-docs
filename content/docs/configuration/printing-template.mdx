**Configurations → Printing template** controls how new labels are produced for your whole organization.

<Screenshot src="/images/configuration/printing-template.png" alt="Printing Template page" />

## Organization printing settings

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Use PrintWise Cloud Printing** | *When enabled, labels are sent to PrintWise for cloud printing. When disabled, the desktop application is used.* See [Printing](/docs/printing). |
| **Disable actions if agent offline** | *When enabled, all printing actions (create shipment, return, ship, reprint, PDF, etc.) will be disabled if the agent is offline.* Prevents buying labels that can't be printed. |

## ZPL settings

**ZPL Stock Type** \* — the thermal label size:

- 4 × 6 Label
- 4 × 6.75 Label (also with leading or trailing document tab)
- 4 × 8 Label
- 4 × 8.5 Label with trailing document tab
- 4 × 9 Label (also with leading or trailing document tab)
- 4 × 10.5 Label with trailing document tab

## PDF settings

| Setting | Options |
| --- | --- |
| **Predefined Printing Template** | The PDF label layout — for example Default or BTO Template. Your organization's custom templates are also listed. |
| **PDF Stock Type** \* | The page size for PDF labels (for example 4 × 6 Page). |
| **Only Download PDF** | When on, PDF labels are downloaded instead of being sent to a printer. |

<Callout>
  Whether a carrier account produces PDF or ZPL is set per account on [Carrier settings](/docs/configuration/carriers/managing-carriers#label-formats).
</Callout>
