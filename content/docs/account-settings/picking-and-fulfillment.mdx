---
title: Picking & fulfillment
description: Settings that control how warehouse staff pick and fulfil orders.
---

Go to **Account settings → Picking & fulfillment**.

<Screenshot src="/images/account-settings/acct-picking.png" alt="Picking & fulfillment settings" />

| Setting | What it does | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Print Shopify order notes on packing slips** | Includes the note from the original Shopify order on the packing slip used for picking. Applies immediately — no reprocessing needed. | Off |

See [Slips, labels and exports](/docs/shipments/slips-labels-and-exports) for printing packing slips.
